# Demo Questions

Build the sample store and run the application with `DEMO_MODE=true` before using these prompts. Ask one question at a time in the main Q&A screen.

## Reference Law

1. What protection does Article 21 of the Constitution provide?
2. What does Article 300A say about deprivation of property?
3. What remedy can a consumer seek for defective goods or deficient services?
4. What is the basic purpose of the Right to Information Act?

## Case Law

1. Find a similar case involving dismissal from service without a proper hearing.
2. Explain the main issue and outcome in the demo service-law case.

## Uploaded Document

Upload `sample_data/uploaded_document_demo.txt`, then ask:

1. What is the main legal issue in this document?
2. Which legal provision is mentioned?
3. What next step does the document suggest?

The demo corpus is intentionally small. A question outside these sample topics may correctly return that there is not enough supporting material.
